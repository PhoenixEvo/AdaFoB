## 1. Executive Conclusion

**Verdict and Scientific Position.** Historical claims *both* supporting AdaFoB (target-supervised LoRA rank improvements, unverified PBA boosts) *and* rejecting AdaFoB (`eval_lora.py`, `gate_d_adafob_eval.py`, `phase4_validation.csv`, and Route B's `protected`/`Q1` gates) fail independent methodological audit. Specifically, the original AdaFoB Prompt Budget Allocator (PBA) was never validly tested in the codebase because five compounding bugs (**Bugs A1–A5**) collapsed prompts into a `13.50 deg` contour arc (`mean budget_Np = 1.216`), silently dropped `"scale": 6` in favor of `"nu": 0.02` (`179/200` slices reduced to zero negatives), deactivated leak-risk weighting (`l_s` drop `0.004988` on z-score CT because of `sigma_I = 20.0`), overwrote learned heatmaps with unsorted points before `SPR`, and polluted FoB baselines with `use_skeleton=True` (`GAPGenerator`). Likewise, Route B's subsequent `INCONCLUSIVE` / `NO-EXPANSION` rejection of AdaFoB candidate selection was confounded by three flaws (**Flaws B1–B3**): evaluating on `cv0` supervoxel-seen patients (`image_8, 14, 20`) on untrained small base organs (`full10` `image_8` pancreas CC Dice `0.013807 -> 0.0`), applying an area-asymmetric veto (`af <= lf` in `experiments/utility_feasibility_v1/scoring_v1.py:70-72`) that blocked strongly negative net-effect points (`case7.npz` `id=3`, `68:1` expansion ratio, net `a_3 = -0.00041928`) and reduced `protected` (`0.668816`) below unprotected `signed` (`0.712946`, which beat every comparator on check `image_20`), and normalizing prototype similarity against whole-slice extracorporeal air (`Q1 = 0.664592`).

At the same time, clean CPU verification of the Step 9 frozen SAM ViT-B (`R1`) controls (`120` episodes, `25` patients, `4,009` slices, `measurements.csv` `sha256: cf8316f5...`) establishes two rigorous baselines that constrain any future claim:
1. Over-prompting with `full10` uniform negative points degrades **Raw** Dice below zero negatives (`positive0`): `78.866361%` vs `80.286251%` (`-1.419890 pp` patient-balanced), generating `6,669.69` detached false-positive voxels per patient that 2D positive-anchored connected components (`CC`) must strip (`-11,299.94 FP / -431.92 TP`) to reach `81.821210%` CC Dice.
2. A simple zero-learning 2-point boundary-proximity rule from the existing FoB candidate pool (`boundary_near2`) improves patient-balanced CC Dice by `+1.342792 pp` over `full10` (`95% CI [+0.637372, +2.104989]`), beats 5-seed `random2_mean5` by `+0.510474 pp` (`[+0.179706, +0.903786]`), reduces HD95 by `-3.423359 mm` (`[-6.804175, -0.155956]`), preserves recall (`-0.140270 pp`, `[-0.468324, +0.157107]`), and closes `28.908989%` (`95% CI [19.825285%, 40.278690%]`) of the non-deployable `GT_greedy_reference` ceiling gap.

**Decision (`REQUEST A BOUNDED NEW EXPERIMENT`).** Neither prior AdaFoB approval nor prior AdaFoB rejection was experimentally clean. Rather than abandoning the hypothesis prematurely or accepting post-hoc narrative rescues, the smallest defensible action is one pre-registered, bug-fixed comparative experiment on frozen SAM ViT-B (`R1`, strict patient-separated Setting I) testing whether a corrected boundary/local-contrast negative-point selector beats matched `boundary_near2` without violating recall tolerance (`>= -0.15 pp` vs `full10`). Until the owner supplies the missing SAM ViT-B checkpoint and authorized NIfTI volumes and grants explicit execution approval, all new inference remains blocked and no submission claim is warranted.
